# KnetSentry Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want a welcoming, technically rigorous, and respectful environment for contributors, security researchers, users, and maintainers, regardless of experience, background, or identity. KnetSentry follows the spirit of the [KDE Community Code of Conduct](https://kde.org/code-of-conduct/); it is **not** an official KDE project.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence respectfully, including when disagreeing about security architecture or risk.
- Credit original authors; be candid about uncertainty and test limitations.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish private logs, live packet captures, credentials, addresses, or vulnerability exploit details without authorization.
- Respect review decisions, maintainers' time, and contributors' boundaries; offer constructive, actionable feedback.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, hateful or discriminatory statements, threats, stalking, sexualized intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, impersonation, deliberate disruption, and unauthorized security testing are not acceptable. Do not use security claims, vulnerability findings, or pull requests as a means of intimidation or coercion.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to KnetSentry repositories, discussions, issue/PR interactions, and project-organized communication. Maintainers may request edits, moderate comments, restrict participation, or remove contributions for serious or repeated misconduct, using a proportional response and documenting the decision when safely possible. Good-faith appeals can be requested through a private channel; the same maintainer may be unable to offer independent adjudication until additional trusted maintainers exist.

## Reporting conduct concerns

**Do not submit a public issue containing identifying harassment reports or private contact details.** A dedicated confidential conduct-reporting address is **not configured yet**. Until one is established, request a private contact method from the maintainer through [the maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/SpilledMilkFactory) without posting sensitive details; for harassment or abuse on GitHub, use GitHub's own reporting and blocking controls. If the maintainer is implicated, prefer GitHub's platform reporting mechanisms rather than disclosing details to the person involved. The maintainer must configure and test a separate confidential channel before claiming such reporting is fully operational.

Security vulnerabilities have a different private intake route: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
